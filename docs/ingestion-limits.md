# Ingestion limits and fixture evidence

The ingestion path is deterministic PDF text extraction plus the existing
chunker. It does **not** include OCR, and the synthetic fixtures are not
clinical evidence or publication material.

## Parsing limits

- **No OCR.** A page is reported in `needsOcrPages`, persisted on the document
  and shown in the UI only when it has fewer than 40 non-whitespace characters
  after page cleanup **and** displayed image coverage of at least 50% of the
  page area. Near-empty chapter dividers, figure/caption pages and other short
  text pages without a dominant image are no longer labelled image-only. The
  detection is heuristic: a low-text page with a large logo or a mostly vector
  drawing can still be classified differently from a true scan. Flagged pages
  are not indexed.
- **No OCR retry loop.** Retrying Parse on an image-only PDF will not create
  text. A separate OCR step is required before relying on those pages.
- **Reading order** uses full-width block separators and left/right geometry.
  Pages with sidebars, three or more columns, overlapping text boxes, or
  unusual rotations can still be ordered incorrectly.
- **Tables** become markdown only when PyMuPDF detects a ruled table. Borderless
  or image-only tables may not be detected. Tables remain page-scoped; a table
  continued across pages is represented by separate page-level blocks rather
  than one merged table. PyMuPDF's cell extraction can collapse some intra-cell
  spaces even when the row/column structure is retained.
- **Headers and footers** are removed when normalized lines repeat in the first
  or last two lines of at least half the pages, or when they match a page-number
  pattern. An irregular or page-specific header/footer may remain.
- **Hyphenation** joins a line-end hyphen before lowercase text
  (`creati-\nng` → `creating`) and preserves inline and compound hyphens
  (`follow-up`, `TABLE-ROW-1`). PDFs that encode a discretionary hyphen inside a
  word can still normalize differently from the visual word.
- **Ligatures** `ﬀ ﬁ ﬂ ﬃ ﬄ ﬅ ﬆ` are expanded. Other Unicode compatibility
  characters are not generally rewritten.
- **Rotated pages** rely on the PDF's rotation metadata and text blocks. Visual
  fidelity is not guaranteed for scanned or heavily transformed pages.
- **Front-matter detection** requires at least two indicators and a clinical
  guard. The current fixture precision is 1.0, but this is a small synthetic
  sample; it is not evidence of precision on a broad guideline corpus.

## Artifact normalization and re-parse

- **HTML entities** in body text and table cells are decoded repeatedly until stable, so stored text contains no `&amp;`, `&lt;`, `&gt;` or `&#` artifacts. This is checked on synthetic fixtures and on the local real-PDF spot check.
- **Footnote markers** are detected from PyMuPDF superscript flags, size and baseline. A single alphabetic marker is removed from visible text and stored as page/offset/context metadata on the document; numeric superscripts such as `mm3` remain visible because they carry meaning. The verifier tolerates the removed marker when matching an exact quote.
- **Wrapped dashes** such as `antigen– positive` are joined to `antigen–positive` by the same normalization function used by the parser, chunker and verifier. Ordinary spaced separators and ranges are left alone.
- **Re-parse** re-extracts an existing PDF and compares pages. Only chunks overlapping a changed page are marked `ReReviewRequired` and lose stale vectors; unchanged reviews remain. This is non-destructive: a failed re-parse keeps the previous text and chunks.
- **Verification** uses one canonical text view on both source and quote: NFKC (so `mm³` equals `mm3`), entity decoding, whitespace/dash/punctuation normalization and parser-recorded footnote markers. The quote must be an exact substring after canonicalization; numbers, negations, strength, scope and topic remain separate checks.
- **Table evidence** is parsed into row label, column headers and cell values. A table quote may be one exact row label plus one exact cell and is displayed with its headers; a paraphrased table sentence is rejected.

## Real guideline PDF

A legally usable, non-copyrighted real guideline PDF has not been supplied in
the repository. The synthetic fixtures are generated on demand and are not
committed; they are the only committed ingestion evidence in this worktree. A
locally held, non-committed real guideline was also spot-checked: its
p.11/p.27/p.40/p.41-style recommendation/table layouts produced no stored
entities, no fused `Screeningc` marker and no `antigen– positive` wrap, and
alphabetic footnote markers were separated into metadata. This is a layout
spot check, not a broad-corpus accuracy claim. If an authorized owner supplies
a PDF, run it through the parse and chunk path, record page-level observations,
and do not commit the PDF.
